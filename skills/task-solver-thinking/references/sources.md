## Corrections and uncertainty in the supplied source plan

- `agentic-kaggle-skill` was supplied without an owner or URL. The FrankS-IntelLab repository is a verified name match and a plausible intended source; the user's intended owner remains unconfirmed.
- The inspected Kaggle and shepsci repositories do not substantiate the combined claim of “66+ ML sub-skills.” This suite makes no such claim.
- The inspected VoltAgent data-scientist profile recommends proper validation but does not supply the detailed zero-leakage protocol attributed to it in the plan. It also includes generic statistical thresholds; those are not treated as universal requirements here.
- Upstream module totals change, and some repositories display inconsistent totals in different sections. The suite relies on inspected capabilities rather than repeating catalog counts.
- Version 1's TRIZ reference was a practical adaptation for sprint decisions without a complete principle catalog. Version 2 adds the forty conventional principle names and original prompts, as documented below; neither version promises that every trade-off can be removed.

## Judging and presentation sources

[Devpost's submission and judging guidance](https://info.devpost.com/blog/understanding-hackathon-submission-and-judging-criteria) explains that organizers set the criteria and highlights implementation, usability, demonstration, impact, idea quality and design. It also directs participants to check eligibility, link accessibility and video requirements. This supports the suite's rule-to-evidence mapping and demo-readiness checks; it does not establish universal weights or mandatory video lengths.

[Devpost's interviews with hackathon judges](https://info.devpost.com/blog/hackathon-judging-tips) describe how judges use videos, functional testing, requirements and technical review. They support rehearsing a clear working demonstration and making the submission understandable without access to the team's internal context.

## Maintenance boundary

Verify event-specific rules and current tool documentation when using the suite. Keep externally reported results separate from locally measured results, and label illustrative numbers as examples. Revisit this ledger when replacing a source or importing upstream material; importing files requires checking their actual notices and license terms rather than relying solely on this summary.
